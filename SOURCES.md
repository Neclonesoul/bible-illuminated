Bible Illuminated includes or presents text associated with the King James Version (Authorized Version) of the Bible.

The legal status of the Authorized Version varies by jurisdiction.

In particular, rights in the Authorized (King James) Version remain vested in the Crown in the United Kingdom and are administered by the Crown's patentee.

Accordingly, this repository does not claim universal public-domain status for the Authorized Version and does not purport to grant rights in that text.
