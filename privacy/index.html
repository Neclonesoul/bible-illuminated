Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to Bible Illuminated, including the Android application and the web application at kjv.tysonbarnes.co.uk.

Personal information

Bible Illuminated does not require an account, registration or sign-in.

Bible Illuminated is designed so that reading preferences, bookmarks and reading state can remain on the user's device.

Advertising

Bible Illuminated does not contain advertising.

Analytics and tracking

Bible Illuminated does not intentionally use advertising trackers or behavioural profiling.

Network access

The application connects to its production website to retrieve application files and Bible content and to support its offline-first operation.

Local storage

Application preferences and features such as bookmarks or reading position may use storage on the user's device so that the application can remember those choices.

Third-party platforms

Android distribution may be provided through Google Play. Google may process information independently according to its own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when application functionality or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this address.
